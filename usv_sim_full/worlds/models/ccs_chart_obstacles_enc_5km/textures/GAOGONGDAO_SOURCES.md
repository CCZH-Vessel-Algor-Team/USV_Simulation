# Connected harbor textures

Rock textures derive from the bundled CC0 Poly Haven `rock_face_03_diff_1k.jpg`; land and foliage are color-adjusted copies of existing CCS assets. See SOURCES.md for the original CC0 licenses. Concrete, roof, paint, roughness and tangent-space normals are deterministic local procedural bakes. The satellite screenshot is a layout reference only; no satellite imagery is redistributed.
